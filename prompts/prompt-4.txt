Organize notes into two classes and control how they show.

Overall notes: a card above the report header and the first group in the panel. It has a rating strip and any number of text notes. Per person the strip allows one thumb, one star rating, and one marker, using the same hover flyouts and clear-on-click rule as the rail. Add an overall note text box in the panel. Show nothing in the report footer.

Inline notes: everything anchored in the report, as built.

One three-way control in the app bar: Hidden, Margin, Inline. H cycles; the choice is remembered.
- Hidden removes chips and highlight colors; the panel still works.
- Margin shows chips in the right gutter.
- Inline renders each note as a callout under its block, clickable to edit. Highlights without text get a short callout showing their color.

Print: a Print button offers "with notes" or "without notes" and opens an in-page preview of all pages. With notes uses the inline callouts and the overall card above the header.

Stamps and highlights may carry text; that text appears everywhere the note does.

# Prompts 1 to 6, paraphrased for slides

**1 · Foundation.** Build a clickable prototype of note-taking on the sample report, as one HTML file. Notes anchor to a heading plus quoted text, never to page structure. Logged-in users only, no speech-to-text, every action in one or two gestures. Deliver just an app bar, a notes panel that toggles with N, and seeded sample notes. Do not invent ways to add notes yet.

**2 · Inline annotations.** A hover rail beside any block offers thumbs, stars, markers, and note. Flyouts show values; only a click sets one. Same value removes, new value replaces, one stamp per family per block per user. Notes get a small editor under the block. Each note becomes a margin chip and a row in the panel, grouped by page and heading. Store enough to survive report changes; unmatched notes go to Unplaced.

**3 · Highlighting.** Selecting text by any means shows a bar with four colors and "highlight and note". One click highlights. Clicking a highlight opens a card to recolor, annotate, or remove. Anchor by the quote plus a little text on each side. Double-clicking a word in an accordion heading must not toggle the section, without delaying the first click.

**4 · Two classes of notes and how they show.** An overall card above the report with per-person ratings and free-text notes. One control for Hidden, Margin, or Inline, cycled with H and remembered. Print with or without notes through an in-page preview. Stamps and highlights can carry text, shown everywhere the note appears.

**5 · Sharing and multiple interviewers.** Notes start private; the owner can share or make private. Nobody can change anyone else's note, and the update and delete functions refuse it whoever calls them. A "Viewing as" switch with three sample interviewers and seeded notes for each. Every note keeps the fields a later AI summary would need, but build no summary.

**6 · Review, fix, publish.** Check the prototype against its goal on a three-block sample: one or two gestures, nothing covers the text, all selection methods raise the bar, "Viewing as" hides and locks correctly. Fix only what fails, re-check only that, finish in five minutes. Then publish the page as a Claude artifact with a handoff note and return the link.

**Lecture trailer on every prompt.** One desktop layout, verify by sampling, 60 seconds per browser check, no screenshots, short replies. Shown separately so the real prompt stays visible above it.
